BiRefNet Lite 512x512 ONNX

This local general-purpose foreground segmentation model is an ONNX re-export of ZhengPeng/BiRefNet_lite, published by Studio Ludens as studioludens/birefnet-lite-512. It is selected for general-object cutouts; the separate MODNet model remains available for portrait matting.

Source model: https://huggingface.co/ZhengPeng/BiRefNet_lite

The pinned 512x512 export is bundled as ONNX fp16 weights with fp32 inputs and outputs (`onnx/model_fp16.onnx`, 94 MiB). The FP32 duplicate is not bundled. The 512px export reduces memory versus the upstream 1024px browser export; fine detail on large images may be limited by its model input resolution.
